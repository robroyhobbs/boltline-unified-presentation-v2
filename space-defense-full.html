Use cases

What space and defense teams use Boltline for

From the boltline.com use cases page, space and defense teams need security, traceability, and audit-readiness for mission-critical systems. These are the specific operational problems Boltline solves.

01

Design-to-build traceability

Track every part from engineering BOM through manufacturing BOM to as-built configuration. Boltline manages eBOM and aBOM with full revision history and part relationships.

Replaces: Spreadsheet BOMs that don't propagate engineering changes. Manual reconciliation before audits.
02

Configuration management

Capture as-designed, as-built, and as-maintained states. Boltline's logical designators link system models to installed hardware with calibration values, serial numbers, and sensor/actuator mapping.

Replaces: Configuration data scattered across disconnected tools with no single view of what's installed.
03

Compliance and audit readiness

ITAR-compliant cloud. Complete digital thread from purchase order to installed part. Full revision history and as-built records generated automatically — not reconstructed manually.

Replaces: Weeks of manual data gathering before customer or government audits. Paper trails that don't connect.
04

Build execution across sites

Digital work plans replace paper travelers. App-like experience with instructions, images, checklists, and triggered workflows. No-code automation standardizes execution across locations.

Replaces: Paper-based processes, tribal knowledge that stays at one location, inconsistent quality across shifts.
How it works

Design, build, test, trace, improve

Boltline's product covers the full hardware lifecycle in five stages. These are the actual modules from boltline.com/product, mapped to the space and defense workflow.

01

Design

Design with CAD, sketches, or existing parts. Build engineering BOMs with component relationships, revisions, and a searchable parts library.

Parts Library eBOM
02

Build

Manufacture and assemble with digital work plans — app-like procedures with instructions, images, and checklists. No-code workflows standardize shop orders across locations.

Work Plans Workflows
03

Test

Confirm what works and what doesn't. QR-code scanning records each part installation and test result in real time. Nonconformance management tracks issues.

QR Scanning Inventory
04

Trace

Formalize parts, assemblies, and final products. As-built BOMs with full configuration state — calibration values, serial numbers, installation locations, and parent/child traceability.

Configuration Management aBOM
05

Improve

Refine products before, during, or after delivery. Complete digital thread from order to installation enables continuous iteration without losing traceability.
