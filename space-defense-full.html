Use cases

What space and defense teams use Boltline for

Security, traceability, and audit-readiness for mission-critical systems.

01

Design-to-build traceability

  • Track every part from eBOM through mBOM to as-built configuration
  • Full revision history and part relationships
  • Changes propagate automatically — no manual reconciliation
02

Configuration management

  • Capture as-designed, as-built, and as-maintained states
  • Logical designators link system models to installed hardware
  • Calibration values, serial numbers, and sensor/actuator mapping
03

Compliance and audit readiness

  • ITAR-compliant cloud infrastructure
  • Complete digital thread from purchase order to installed part
  • As-built records generated automatically — not reconstructed for audits
04

Build execution across sites

  • Digital work plans replace paper travelers
  • App-like experience with instructions, images, and checklists
  • No-code automation standardizes execution across locations
