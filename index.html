Act 1 / Positioning arc
05 / 19

The breakthrough came when the language moved up a level.

V1

"Never write docs again."

  • Technically true, strategically weak
  • Collapsed us into the AI docs crowd
  • Could not reach executive buyers
V2

"Documentation is an engineering tax."

  • Better pain framing
  • Still too tactical
  • Useful, but not category-defining
V3

"Every business has a Knowledge Fabric. Yours is broken."

  • Moved from output to system truth
  • Worked with devs, execs, and enterprise partners
  • Created language the market could remember

The lesson I carried forward: when the category is still forming, the marketer has to discover the right abstraction layer, not just write better copy.

Best signal phrase: "drift is a silent tax."

Act 2 / First move
08 / 19

Measurement before campaigns.

Built first

4-layer attribution model

  • Channel: where demand came from
  • Method: how the touch happened
  • Source: the origin point worth comparing
  • Sub-source: enough granularity to improve over time
Why first

Because opinion loses power when the scoreboard is trusted.

Once the source architecture existed, future launches, reporting, and budget discussions stopped being debates.

Lead source coverage moved from 65% to 96%, and the model was durable enough that new vertical launches worked on day one.

When leadership asked whether marketing was working, the answer lived in the dashboard, not in my rhetoric.

This is the exact instinct I would bring to Boltline

Act 3 / How I execute
16 / 19

The difference is learning velocity, not campaign volume.

Most marketing teams run 20-30 experiments a year. The system I build runs that in a week. Same loop everywhere: modify one variable, deploy, measure, keep or discard, repeat.

The loop

One pattern across every channel.

  • Outbound: test subject, opener, CTA — score in 72 hours
  • Ad creative and landing pages — score in 7-14 days
  • Sales assets and onboarding — score in 30-90 days
The compound effect

Each experiment makes every channel smarter.

  • Outreach learns which pain framing gets replies
  • That language feeds ad copy, call scripts, and content
  • The system builds a proprietary map of what resonates with Boltline's ICP
Traditional pace
~30/yr

experiments per year at most teams

With autonomous loops
2,000+/yr

continuous testing across every growth surface

The real moat
The graph

experiment history is proprietary — competitors can't copy what they never ran

AI models will become commodities. The moat is the experiment history — a system that has run thousands of tests against your specific ICP and knows what works.

Optimize learning velocity, not campaign count
