Act 2 / First move
08 / 20

Measurement before campaigns.

Built first

4-layer attribution model

  • Channel: where demand came from
  • Method: how the touch happened
  • Source: the origin point worth comparing
  • Sub-source: enough granularity to improve over time
Why first

Because opinion loses power when the scoreboard is trusted.

Once the source architecture existed, future launches, reporting, and budget discussions stopped being debates.

Lead source coverage moved from 65% to 96%, and the model was durable enough that new vertical launches worked on day one.

When leadership asked whether marketing was working, the answer lived in the dashboard, not in my rhetoric.

This is the exact instinct I would bring to Boltline

Act 3 / How I execute
16 / 20

Learning speed matters more than campaign count.

The loop is simple: test one variable, deploy fast, measure, keep or discard, then feed the learning into the next asset.

The loop

One pattern across every channel.

  • Outbound: subject, opener, CTA
  • Ad creative and landing pages — score in 7-14 days
  • Sales assets and onboarding — score in 30-90 days
The compound effect

Each experiment makes every channel smarter.

  • Outreach learns which pain framing gets replies
  • That language feeds ad copy, call scripts, and content
  • The real asset is a proprietary map of what resonates
Optimize for signal quality and learning speed, not activity volume.
